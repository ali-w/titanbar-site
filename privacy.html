Titanbar privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

What Titanbar does with your data

  • Google Tasks. When you type a to-do or shopping command, Titanbar asks you to grant access to your Google Tasks (the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks scope). It uses that access only to find or create the lists "To-do" and "Shopping" and to add the task you typed. It does not read, show, change or delete any other task or list content.
  • Contacts. If you use the contact commands, Titanbar reads your device contacts on the phone to find a name and number. Contacts are not uploaded or stored anywhere by Titanbar.
  • Everything else. App search, timers, alarms, calendar reminders and notes are handled by other apps on your phone through standard Android actions.

What Titanbar collects and shares

Titanbar has no servers. The developer does not receive, collect, store or sell any of your data. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. Titanbar keeps no copy of your tasks, and no sign-in token is stored by the app: it asks Google for a short-lived access token each time it is needed. The text of a task you type is sent directly from your phone to Google's Tasks service and nowhere else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Titanbar's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can remove Titanbar's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
